## Fixed decision rule
Independent unit: note. At least three of four votes makes an A/B win; 2–2 is a tie. Primary estimand is A win probability conditional on a decisive note. Ties and their rate are reported, not silently counted as independent judgments. Exact two-sided binomial test against .5, alpha .05, once at n=150: PROVEN for significant A superiority; DISPROVEN for significant B superiority. Exact 95% CI reported. Equivalence: exact TOST at alpha .05, equivalently 90% Clopper–Pearson CI wholly inside [.45,.55], gives DISPROVEN (equivalent within margin). Five percentage points is the largest negligible preference advantage given the added drafting cost. No optional peeking or widening the margin. Latency includes model loading and drafting+polish; paired bootstrap 95% CI over notes for mean difference and ratio, 10,000 resamples, seed 2803.

## Power and feasibility
Quick normal approximation: n≈(1.96+.842)^2*.25/.10^2=197 for 80% power at p=.60; n=150 has roughly 69% power there and >95% at p=.65. At the previous 23/39≈.59, power is only about 59%. Equivalence at true .50 requires roughly ((1.645+1.282)*.5/.05)^2=857 for 80% power; 150 cannot establish the requested narrow band even at 75/150. Four judgments do not turn 150 notes into 600 independent trials. Thus the requested fixed n and equivalence margin cannot guarantee a conclusive outcome. We retain both explicitly, and will not fabricate certainty. A residual uncertainty outcome is STILL INCONCLUSIVE when the fixed sample/compute/time budget is exhausted.
