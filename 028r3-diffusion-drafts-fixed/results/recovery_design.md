Fixed decision rule: one final look, exact two-sided binomial test at alpha .05 on A wins among decisive notes. PROVEN if significant and A wins >50%; DISPROVEN if significant and A wins <50%. If nonsignificant, exact TOST at alpha .05 for conditional A win probability in [.45,.55], equivalent to the 90% Clopper–Pearson CI lying strictly within the band; passing means DISPROVEN (equivalent within margin). Five percentage points is the user-specified largest negligible preference shift. Also report 95% exact CI. No treating nonsignificance as equivalence. Latency: paired note-level bootstrap (20,000 resamples, seed 2803), mean A–B seconds with 95% percentile CI and mean ratio; diagnostic, not a substitute quality endpoint.
